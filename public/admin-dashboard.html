                Demo data. Every figure is generated by a seeded model of a Melbourne homewares store and changes consistently with the period: revenue = orders × average order value, the funnel ends at the order count, and margins come from the unit costs in the table above.

                Definitions. Net revenue is sales after WELCOME10 discounts and in-home-trial refunds, ex GST. Gross margin is net revenue less product cost, freight (outbound, trial returns and replacements, net of freight charged and White Glove fees), crating and packing, and Square fees. Net profit adds breakage-cover income ($12.50 option) and deducts marketing and $24,000 a month of fixed costs. LTV is average order value × gross margin × 1.55 lifetime orders.
