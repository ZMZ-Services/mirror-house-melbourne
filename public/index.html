Common Questions

Hanging, Delivery & FAQ

How are large floor mirrors delivered safely?

Every floor and wall mirror travels upright in a custom reinforced timber crate with foam corner guards and shock-absorbing blocks, carried by a dedicated fragile-freight courier. You'll get SMS tracking before dispatch.

What is the Transit & Breakage Cover option?

For a flat $12.50, selected at checkout, we send a priority replacement at no extra cost if your order arrives damaged. Just send us photos within 48 hours of delivery.

Is hanging hardware included, and how should I hang my mirror?

Yes. Wall mirrors come with D-rings or a heavy-gauge French cleat. For anything over 10kg, fix into wall studs or masonry, never plasterboard alone. If you're unsure, a licensed handyman can hang it in under an hour.

Can I book White Glove delivery?

Yes. Choose White Glove at checkout and a two-person team will carry your mirror into the room of your choice, unpack it and take away all the crating.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Checkout runs securely through Square. We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay and Google Pay, with no card surcharge.
