Common Questions

Hanging, Delivery & FAQ

How are large floor mirrors delivered safely?

Every floor and wall mirror is packed in a heavy-duty cardboard box with protective foam, carried by a dedicated fragile-freight courier. You'll get SMS tracking before dispatch.

What if my mirror arrives damaged?

Wherever possible, we check each mirror with you on delivery so we can confirm together that it has arrived in perfect condition. If anything isn't quite right, please let us know straight away (photos help) and we'll replace it promptly at no cost to you.

Is hanging hardware included, and how should I hang my mirror?

Yes. Wall mirrors come with D-rings or a heavy-gauge French cleat. For anything over 10kg, fix into wall studs or masonry, never plasterboard alone. If you're unsure, a licensed handyman can hang it in under an hour.
